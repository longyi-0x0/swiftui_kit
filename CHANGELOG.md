## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `GlassCapsuleBar`: a floating capsule bar with items, an optional search
  field, and an optional trailing action.
- `GlassTitleBar`: a floating title bar with an inline or large title and
  leading, principal, and trailing items, optionally grouped into a single
  glass.
- `GlassIcon`: pairs a Flutter `IconData` with the matching SF Symbol.
- `GlassRenderer` and `GlassRendererScope`: choose between the native SwiftUI
  views, the Flutter fallback, or automatic selection per platform.
